# Surface brief: Huddle landing and shell

Scope: landing page (persuade) and the product shell it previews (operate).
Source of direction: the owner pinned it. He rejected formula-derived directions and asked for study of 50-100 real sites, then an original language. This overrides the seed roll (3328256e).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The only colour on screen belongs to people. The page is black and white until someone is there. Refuses the centred headline, two pill buttons and a screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white / pure black ground, no brand accent. Eight person inks. Science Gothic, variable width 50-200, set edge to edge. Name-tag shape (one sharp top-left corner, the rest round) taken from a cursor label. Schibsted Grotesk for reading.

STORY: Letters make room for whoever arrives; visitor sees presence before reading a feature, adds a teammate, then watches a shared page get written.

FIRST VIEWPORT: slim nav; two justified lines "WRITE IT / TOGETHER" filling the width; demo teammate cursors and the visitor's pointer swell the letters they touch and colour them while neighbours compress; below, one sentence, primary action "Start writing", secondary "Add a demo teammate".

FORM: reactive justified type with conserved line width. Later surfaces inherit it as: presence colours blocks, avatars and selections; chrome stays monochrome.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
